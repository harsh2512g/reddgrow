# Responsible use

ThreadSignal is an independent product, not affiliated with Reddit. It helps people discover public conversations and prepare evidence-backed replies. A real person must review and publish each reply, follow community rules and disclose their relationship to any recommended product. ThreadSignal cannot guarantee moderator acceptance, account safety, search rankings or AI citations.

The notice is shown during onboarding/settings and must be accepted before the first draft approval. Consent to the notice does not bypass a blocked claim, unsafe opportunity, missing affiliation or stale evidence.

The extension uses `activeTab` only after a person invokes it. Unsupported, ambiguous or nonempty editors fail safely and offer explicit copy. It does not read unrelated browsing or scrape the discussion. A manually recorded publication permalink is a person's declaration, not verification that Reddit accepted a comment.

## Before real Reddit ingestion

Stay in mock mode until the intended use has the required access/permission and the owner approves new personal configuration. Re-check current [Reddit Developer Terms](https://redditinc.com/policies/developer-terms), [Data API Terms](https://redditinc.com/policies/data-api-terms), and [API guidance](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/16160319875092-Reddit-Data-API-Wiki). These links identify launch review work; this phase did not verify legal eligibility or activate a Reddit account.

Respect provider rate-limit/reset and authorization pauses. Never substitute another identity, proxy or scraper when access is denied. Apply deletion synchronization and minimal retention. Live Reddit editor compatibility remains manual QA under the [extension guide](extension.md). An operator may pause misuse, but platform-admin status does not authorize casually reading private customer knowledge/drafts.
